## ✨ What is eLib?

Textbooks are dense. Novels are layered. Study material rarely explains itself.

**eLib** turns the books you already have into interactive AI mentors. Drop in a PDF or EPUB, and you can read it in a clean, distraction-free reader while chatting with an assistant that has read every page and answers from the book itself, not from guesswork.

Think of it as a patient tutor who has read your textbook cover to cover and is always ready to explain, summarize, or quiz you.

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **Students** working through textbooks, papers, and course readings
- **Self-learners** tackling a tough technical book
- **Readers** who want help untangling a complex novel or nonfiction title
- **Researchers and professionals** who need to find and understand things in long documents fast

## 🚀 Getting started

1. **Open eLib** on the web or in the desktop app.
2. **Upload a book.** PDF or EPUB, up to 50 MB.
3. **Wait a moment** while eLib reads it. You can see its progress in your library, and it's usually quick.
4. **Start reading and asking.** Open the book and use the chat panel beside the reader.

### Tips for better answers

- **Be specific.** "What does the author mean by 'marginal utility' in chapter 3?" beats "Explain this."
- **Highlight first.** Select the confusing paragraph, then ask about it.
- **Follow up.** The chat remembers the conversation, so you can dig deeper.
- **Ask for different formats.** Try "Summarize this chapter in five bullets" or "Quiz me on what I just read."

## ❓ FAQ

**What file types can I upload?**
PDF and EPUB, up to 50 MB each.

**Does it work with scanned PDFs?**
eLib works best with PDFs that contain real, selectable text. Scanned pages that are only images may not be readable by the AI.

**Will the AI make things up?**
eLib answers using passages retrieved from your book, which keeps responses grounded. Like any AI, it can still make mistakes, so double-check anything important against the text.

**Is my book shared with other users?**
No. Your library is private to your account.

**Are my chats saved?**
Conversations are kept for your current session and aren't stored as a permanent history.

**Do I need an account?**
You'll want one to keep your library across devices and to manage your plan.

**How long does processing take?**
Most books are ready within a short time. Larger books take longer, and you can see the status in your library.
